How the Programme Works

A practical 5-step framework focused on regular speaking practice.

1
Attend the free common orientation on 17 October. Get a clear overview of the methodology and meet the mentor.
2
Join your dedicated 8-person batch. Choose between Housewives, Business, or Students to practice with peers having identical goals.
3
Attend 4 live classes per week for 3 months (48 sessions). Interactive small-group sessions with guaranteed speaking time.
4
Practise through short speaking exercises, role-plays, and daily voice-note homework. Receive polite mentor audio feedback between classes.
