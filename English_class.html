12-week speaking journey & batch timings

12-Week Course Plan 12 Weeks • 3x/Wk • 60–75 Min
▾
Wks 1–2

Foundations

Introduce yourself with ease, clear pronunciation & daily routine.

Wks 3–4

Everyday English

Shopping, directions, workplace chats & friendly small talk.

Wks 5–6

Speaking Grammar

Tenses to tell stories, asking questions & fixing common mistakes.

Wks 7–8

Fluency

Share your opinions, join group discussions & hesitate less.

Wks 9–10

Practical English

Job interviews, workplace meetings & short presentations.

Wks 11–12

Public Speaking

Give a prepared talk + your final speaking assessment.
