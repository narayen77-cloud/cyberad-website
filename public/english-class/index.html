Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our batches, timings, monthly fee, and teaching method.

We run three dedicated 8-person micro-batches: Housewives & Homemakers, Business Owners & Freelancers, and Students & Job Seekers. You are placed in the batch that matches your profile so you always practise with peers who share similar daily conversations and goals.
Each batch meets for 4 live classes per week for 3 months (48 sessions total). Homemaker batches typically run in convenient daytime/afternoon slots, while Business Owner and Student batches run in evening slots. Message us on WhatsApp to check the available slot timings for your batch.
The fee is a transparent ₹2,000 per month, paid monthly. There is no registration fee, no hidden charges, and no requirement to pay for all 3 months upfront.
Traditional classes focus heavily on written grammar rules, which often makes adults overthink and hesitate more while speaking. Our method focuses on active speaking practice—using real-life role-plays, guided conversations in an 8-person circle, and daily voice-note feedback so speaking becomes natural.
In large classes, most participants stay on mute and barely get a chance to speak. By capping each batch at 8 learners, everyone gets unhurried speaking turns in every live session along with personal attention from the mentor.
Between your 4 weekly live classes, you receive short daily speaking prompts that you can complete via voice notes for supportive mentor feedback. All session notes, practical sentence templates, and vocabulary guides are organised on Google Classroom so you can review them anytime.
