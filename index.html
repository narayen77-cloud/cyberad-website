Common Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Is this suitable for absolute beginners?

Yes. Zero knowledge of digital marketing is the starting point.

Is this for business owners and for people in marketing?

Yes. Owners, marketing managers, and anyone already doing marketing who needs digital work to bring customers and payment.

I already handle marketing. I just need it to produce more sales. Is this still for me?

Yes. That is a main seat in the room. If you post, run ads, or brief a freelancer and still cannot show how that work becomes enquiries and payment, this programme is for you. We do not start from “what is Instagram.” We start from the path: attention → offer → payment → repeat.

I am a marketing manager, not the owner. Will this help my numbers?

Yes. You leave with an offer written in plain language, a payment link you can use or hand to the owner, and a WhatsApp pitch the team can send. The point is more of the right customers — not a better content calendar with no path to money.

What language will the training be conducted in?

The programme is conducted bilingually in English & Tamil (தமிழ் & English). Complex concepts are broken down into simple, practical business language with local examples so every participant can follow with ease.

Will I become a digital marketer in three days?

No. You will see the whole journey and run the first practical pieces yourself.

Do I need a big ad budget?

No. You need a business (or responsibility for its marketing), honesty, and three days of work.

Will I get a certificate?

Yes — for completing the programme. The useful part is the offer, the payment link, and the pitch.

What this programme will not do

It will not promise overnight sales or a magic algorithm. It will not turn three days into a full-time media career. It will not replace the work you still have to do with your own customers after you leave.
