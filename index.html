Common Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Is this suitable for absolute beginners?

Yes. The program is designed assuming zero prior experience. We start from foundations and build practical skills step by step.

I’m a business owner — is this useful for me?

Very much. Many business owners join specifically to understand digital marketing so they can hire better, brief agencies clearly, evaluate performance reports, and stop wasting money on underperforming marketers.

How much time do I need to commit per week?

Most students spend 6–10 hours per week (modules + live sessions + assignments). The structure is flexible enough for college students, working professionals, and busy business owners.

Will I get a certificate?

Yes. On successful completion you receive a certificate. For college batches we offer co-branded certificates under the institution’s name.

Can colleges run this under their own brand?

Absolutely. We offer full licensing, train-the-trainer packages, and co-branded delivery models so institutions can own the student experience while using our proven system.

What happens after the course ends?

We conduct exit interviews, placement readiness sessions, and follow up at 15 and 45 days. Success stories are shared and advanced/alumni options are offered.
